## Hello and Welcome.

I'm Michel Gonçalves, a Brazilian **Senior Analytics Engineer** with over 6 years of hands-on experience in high-impact analytical environments. 

## My Journey

My path into the data universe started 18 years ago, when I was doing complex statistical analysis on my MSc and PhD degrees. However, it took me some attempts (failures, to be honest) at consulting, sales and entrepreneurship to finally let go of my attempt to live as a biologist and migrate to analytics engineering.

## My Focus Today
It is not X or Y framework or tool. It is delivering good Data Products, and by that I mean data that is:
* Aligned with stakeholders' needs;
* Clearly defined and easy to understand;
* Accurate, complete and fast to query;
* Well-architected and documented.

## My Passion
It is to read several articles weekly to stay updated on what other AEs are thinking and doing. That gives me a feeling of stepping off the isolated 'island' of my daily work and connect with our data community.
